# Code of conduct

LiteDB follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work, not
the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not welcome in
issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

To report a problem, email the maintainer at createdbyadham@gmail.com. Reports
are read by the maintainer only and kept confidential. The maintainer may
remove comments, close threads, or block anyone who does not follow this code.
